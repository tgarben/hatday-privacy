Common questions

How does Hatday know when an offer is available?

Enter your birthday once (month and day only) and Hatday works out each offer's dates for you. Some offers run for your whole birthday month and others for a few days around your birthday. Open any offer to see the exact dates and how many days are left.

How do I change my birthday?

Open the Settings tab and tap Birthday. Every offer's dates update straight away.

How do I add an offer to my tracker, and mark it as used?

Tap the + next to an offer, or open the offer and tap Add to my tracker. In My Tracker, tick the circle beside an offer (or open it and tap Mark as used) once you've claimed it. Used offers move to their own section, and you can tap Used - tap to undo if you ticked it by mistake. Your used offers reset each birthday year.

How do I remove an offer from my tracker?

Open the offer, tap the ••• menu in the top corner, then choose Remove from my tracker.

An offer is wrong, expired or has a broken link.

Open the offer, tap the ••• menu, then choose Report wrong info. You can also use Report Wrong Info in Settings. Pick what's wrong and add a few details, and we'll check it. Offers are set by each company and can change, so it's always worth confirming with the company before you go.

An offer is missing.

Open Settings and tap Suggest a Freebie. You'll also find it at the bottom of the Freebies list and on the "no matches" screen after a search.

Do I need to sign up first?

Many offers need a free rewards account with the company before you can claim them. These show a Signup required note and a Sign up button that takes you to the company's own page.

Can I use Hatday on my iPhone and iPad?

Yes. If you're signed in to iCloud, your birthday, tracked offers and used offers sync between your devices automatically. You can see whether sync is on under iCloud Sync in Settings.

I deleted the app and my list came back. Why?

With iCloud on, Hatday keeps a copy of your birthday and tracker in your own iCloud account, so reinstalling the app restores it. To stop that, turn off iCloud for Hatday, or delete its data, in your device's iCloud settings. We can't see or remove this data, because it's held in your account, not ours.

What information does Hatday collect?

Very little. There are no accounts, and your birthday and tracker stay on your device and in your own iCloud. Read the full details in the privacy policy below.
