Hatday Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

Hatday helps you keep track of free birthday offers. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your device, and the little that is sent to us. Hatday is made by Tyler Garben.

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your birthday (month and day only) and the offers you track stay on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, Apple syncs them between your own devices. We can't see them.
  • The app sends us only two things: anonymous counts of how many people saved or used each offer, and the reports and suggestions you choose to write.
  • We don't show ads, we don't use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we don't track you.

Information that stays on your device

Hatday stores the following on your device:

  • Your birthday (month and day only, no year), used to work out when each offer is available.
  • The offers you add to your tracker, and which of them you've marked as used.
  • Your display preferences, such as how your tracker is sorted.

If you're signed in to iCloud, this information is also stored in your own iCloud account using Apple's key-value storage, so it follows you between your iPhone and iPad. That copy is held by Apple under your Apple account and is governed by Apple's privacy policy. We have no access to it.

Information sent to us

Hatday stores its shared data in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). When you use the app, it sends the following to our Firebase project:

  • Anonymous popularity counts. When you add an offer to your tracker, or mark it used, the app adds 1 to a counter for that offer. Each counter holds only the offer's ID and a total. No name, device identifier, birthday or other personal information is sent with it, so a count can't be traced back to you.
  • Reports. If you tap "Report Wrong Info", we receive the offer concerned, the type of problem you pick, the details you type, the app version and the time you sent it.
  • Suggestions. If you use "Suggest a Freebie", we receive the name, reward, optional website and optional details you type, plus the app version and the time you sent it.

Please don't put personal information, such as your name or contact details, in reports or suggestions. We have no way to tell who sent one.

Like any app that talks to an online service, Hatday's requests reach Google's servers, which receive standard technical information such as your IP address and basic device and app details. Google handles this under its own terms. We don't use it to identify you, and Hatday doesn't combine it with anything else.

How we use information

  • To keep the list of offers accurate: we review reports and suggestions and update the list.
  • To see which offers are popular, using the anonymous counts.
  • To run and improve the app.

We don't sell your information, we don't share it for advertising, and we don't use it to build profiles.

Tracking, ads and analytics

Hatday doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites. It doesn't use your advertising identifier, doesn't show ads, and doesn't contain third-party analytics or advertising software.

How long we keep information

Reports and suggestions are kept for as long as we need them to check and correct the list, and may then be deleted. The anonymous counts are kept as running totals. The information on your device stays until you delete the app. Because reports, suggestions and counts carry no account or device identifier, we can't look up or remove an individual person's contribution. If you'd like something you wrote in a report or suggestion removed, email us with the details and we'll do our best to find and delete it.

Your choices

  • You can change or clear your birthday in Settings, remove any offer from your tracker, or delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • You can turn off iCloud syncing for Hatday in your device's iCloud settings.
  • You can choose not to send reports or suggestions. The app works fully without them.

Links to other websites

Offers link to the companies' own websites and sign-up pages, which open in your browser or the company's app. Those sites are run by the companies, not by us, and have their own privacy practices.

Children

Hatday isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

About the brands in the app

Hatday is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of the brands it lists. All trademarks and logos belong to their respective owners. Offers are set by each company and may change, so check with the company before you go.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be at this address, and the date at the top shows when it last changed. If we ever change what the app collects, we'll update this policy first.
